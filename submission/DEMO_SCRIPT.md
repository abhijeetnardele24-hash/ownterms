# Canonical Demo Video Script

**Duration:** ~2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Narrator Tone:** Professional, authoritative, clear, founder-led.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & The White Space
- **Visual:** Split screen showing an autonomous marketing agent writing copy and generating social ad assets. A popup shows a copyright takedown notice.
- **Audio:** *"Autonomous AI agents are now creating marketing campaigns, generating videos, and training models. But they share one fatal flaw: they scrape copyrighted assets without permission, licenses, or payment rails. If an agent uses your photography or dataset today, you get zero compensation—and the enterprise using that agent faces massive copyright liability."*

### [0:25 - 0:50] Introducing OwnTerms
- **Visual:** Transition to OwnTerms homepage. Clean Ink and Paper aesthetic, highlighting *"Your agent can use it—after it licenses it."*
- **Audio:** *"Meet OwnTerms: machine-readable IP rights licensing for the agentic economy. OwnTerms transforms static digital assets into machine-licensable rights instruments. Creators set the policy; AI agents extract their intended use; our deterministic engine finds the exact license; and PayPal Sandbox settles the transaction in seconds."*

### [0:50 - 1:25] Intent Extraction & Deterministic Matching
- **Visual:** Open asset `asset_night_market_01` ("Night Market Signals" by Mira Rao). Click "Load Demo Request". Click "Extract Rights Scope with AI".
- **Audio:** *"Here is Mira Rao's photograph, 'Night Market Signals'. An autonomous media agent wants to use it with this request: 'Use this photograph in a paid Instagram campaign for 30 days in India and Singapore. Budget up to $80. Do not allow AI training.' Notice what happens: Gemini extracts the factual use parameters—channels, duration, territory, and exclusions. Crucially, the AI has zero power over price or permissions. Our deterministic engine compares those facts against Mira's policies. Editorial is blocked because paid ads are prohibited. Extended is rejected because it exceeds the $80 budget. Commercial Social at $69 is the exact optimal match."*

### [1:25 - 1:55] PayPal Orders v2 Settlement
- **Visual:** Click the PayPal smart button on the $69 card. Sandbox approval modal executes. Backend capture and read-back animation triggers.
- **Audio:** *"With one click, the agent executes checkout through PayPal Sandbox Orders v2. The price is strictly server-enforced—the client cannot alter it. Once PayPal authorizes the capture, OwnTerms performs a server-side read-back check to verify the transaction details."*

### [1:55 - 2:20] Cryptographic Rights Receipt & Anti-Tamper Demo
- **Visual:** Rights Receipt page renders. Canonical SHA-256 hash displayed with green shield. Click "Test Anti-Tampering", flip one character, show hash collision error.
- **Audio:** *"Upon settlement, OwnTerms issues an immutable Rights Receipt. It binds the asset, the license tier, the PayPal capture ID, and granted scope into a canonical SHA-256 hash. If an agent tries to modify a single byte of this JSON receipt—for example, changing 'Commercial' to 'Unlimited'—the cryptographic verification immediately fails."*

### [2:20 - 2:45] AG Grid Creator Control Room & Conclusion
- **Visual:** Switch to `/control-room`. Demonstrate AG Grid sorting, search, revenue summary KPIs, and the "Rights Coverage" custom widget.
- **Audio:** *"Finally, Mira tracks every autonomous license in her Creator Control Room, powered by AG Grid. She can audit every transaction in real-time and view her 'Rights Coverage' matrix to see where her work is deployed globally. OwnTerms connects creators, autonomous agents, and PayPal to build what's next in agentic commerce."*
