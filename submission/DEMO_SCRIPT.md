# Canonical Demo Video Script

**Duration:** ~2 minutes 45 seconds  
**Narrator Tone:** Professional, authoritative, clear, founder-led.

---

### [0:00 - 0:25] The Problem & The White Space
- **Visual:** Split screen showing an autonomous marketing agent generating ad copy and sourcing visual assets. A copyright warning modal highlights legal risk.
- **Audio:** *"Autonomous AI agents are now creating marketing campaigns, generating videos, and training models. But they share one fatal flaw: they use copyrighted assets without permission, licenses, or payment rails. If an agent uses your digital work today, you get zero compensation—and the enterprise using that agent faces severe copyright liability."*

### [0:25 - 0:50] Introducing OwnTerms
- **Visual:** Transition to OwnTerms homepage. Clean Ink and Paper aesthetic, highlighting *"Your agent can use it—after it licenses it."*
- **Audio:** *"Meet OwnTerms: machine-readable IP rights licensing for the agentic economy. OwnTerms transforms static digital creations into machine-licensable rights instruments. Creators set policies; AI agents extract their intended use; our deterministic rule engine finds the exact license; and PayPal Orders v2 settles the transaction."*

### [0:50 - 1:25] Intent Extraction & Deterministic Matching
- **Visual:** Open asset `asset_night_market_01` ("Night Market Signals"). Click "Load Canonical Demo Request". Click "Extract Rights Scope with AI".
- **Audio:** *"Here is an original digital artwork, 'Night Market Signals'. An autonomous media agent wants to use it with this request: 'Use this photograph in a paid Instagram campaign for 30 days in India and Singapore. Budget up to $80. Do not allow AI training.' Notice what happens: Gemini extracts the factual use parameters—channels, duration, territory, and exclusions. Crucially, the AI has zero power over price or permissions. Our deterministic engine compares those facts against creator policies. Editorial is blocked because paid ads are prohibited. Extended is rejected because it exceeds the $80 budget. Commercial Social at $69 is the exact optimal match."*

### [1:25 - 1:55] PayPal Orders v2 Settlement
- **Visual:** Show the checkout rail. Explain the dual-mode architecture: deterministic Fixture mode for automated judge testing and official PayPal Smart Buttons for live Sandbox buyer approval. Click settle license.
- **Audio:** *"The agent executes checkout through PayPal Orders v2. The price is strictly server-enforced—the client cannot alter it. Once capture executes, OwnTerms performs a server-side read-back check to verify the transaction status, amount, and custom reference ID."*

### [1:55 - 2:20] Tamper-Evident Rights Receipt & Anti-Tamper Demo
- **Visual:** Rights Receipt page renders. Canonical SHA-256 digest displayed with green shield. Click "Tamper Price to $0.00", show live red mismatch alert. Click "Restore Authentic State", show green verification.
- **Audio:** *"Upon settlement, OwnTerms issues a tamper-evident Rights Receipt. It binds the asset, the license tier, the PayPal capture ID, and granted scope into a canonical SHA-256 digest. In our interactive testing sandbox, if an adversary tampers with a single byte—for example, zeroing the price—the cryptographic verification immediately flags the tampering."*

### [2:20 - 2:45] AG Grid Creator Control Room & Conclusion
- **Visual:** Switch to `/control-room`. Demonstrate AG Grid sorting, search, provenance breakdown, and the Rights Coverage Matrix showing isolated channel-by-territory saturation.
- **Audio:** *"Finally, creators track every machine clearance in their Control Room, powered by AG Grid Community. They can audit every transaction and inspect their Rights Coverage matrix to see where their work is deployed globally. OwnTerms connects creators, autonomous agents, and PayPal to build what's next in agentic commerce."*
